# FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Dashboard — Rulebook

## Overview
Compete across three separate prediction games to climb the leaderboard. Each game has its own scoring system and rankings. The higher your accuracy, the more points you earn.

---

## GAME 1: GROUP STAGE PREDICTIONS
**Scope:** Predict the outcome of all group stage matches.

**Time Limit:** Predictions must be submitted by match kick-off. No changes allowed after.

**Scoring:**
- Correct result (W/D/L): 10 points
- Correct goal difference: +5 points bonus
- Correct exact score: +10 points bonus

*Example: You predict Brazil 3–0 Mexico. If they win 3–0, you get 10 + 5 + 10 = 25 points. If they win 2–0, you get 10 + 5 = 15 points.*

**Leaderboard:** Ranked by total points. Separate from other games.

---

## GAME 2: KNOCKOUT PREDICTIONS
**Scope:** Predict match outcomes from Round of 16 through Finals.

**Time Limit:** Predictions must be submitted 24 hours before each match.

**Scoring:**
- Correct match winner: 15 points
- Correct goal difference: +5 points bonus
- Correct exact score: +15 points bonus
- Correct penalties outcome (if applicable): +10 points bonus

*The further you progress (semi-finals, finals), the higher the stakes—but scoring stays the same per match.*

**Leaderboard:** Ranked by total points. Separate from Group Stage and Champions games.

---

## GAME 3: CHAMPIONS PREDICTION
**Scope:** Predict the tournament winner and create a "top 4" shortlist.

**The 25% Rule:**
- Select up to 4 teams you think will win
- The closer a team finishes to first place, the more points you earn
- **Scoring:**
  - Winner correctly in your list: 100 points + 50 bonus points for placing them 1st
  - Runner-up in your list: 30 points
  - Semi-finalist in your list: 15 points
  - In your list but eliminated earlier: 5 points

*Why this works: More teams clustered near the top = more points available. Picking the eventual winner + 3 finalists earns more than picking one long-shot.*

**Time Limit:** Final submission 24 hours before the Finals match.

**Leaderboard:** Ranked by total points. Separate from other games.

---

## OTHER AWARDS
Bonus categories tracked separately:

- **Golden Boot:** Predict the tournament's top scorer (name + goal count). 30 points if correct.
- **Best Player:** Predict tournament MVP. 20 points if correct.
- **Dark Horse:** Correctly predict a team eliminated in Round of 16 or Quarters. 5 points per correct prediction.

These do not affect the main leaderboards but appear on an "Achievements" tab.

---

## IMPORTANT NOTES

1. **Three Independent Games:** Your Group Stage rank, Knockout rank, and Champions rank are completely separate. You could win one and place last in another—that's OK.

2. **No Edits After Deadline:** Once a prediction is submitted and the time limit has passed, you cannot change it.

3. **Live Scoring:** Leaderboards update within 1 hour of match completion.

4. **Tiebreaker:** If two players have identical scores, the one who submitted first ranks higher.

5. **Eligibility:** All company employees and contractors are eligible. Tournament rules and FIFA match results are the source of truth.
